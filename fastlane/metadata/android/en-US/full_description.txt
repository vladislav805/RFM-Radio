**RFM Radio** is an offline FM receiver for Android devices with supported Qualcomm Snapdragon radio hardware. It uses the phone's built-in FM tuner instead of streaming stations over the Internet.

**Important:**

* Root access is required.
* Android 6.0 or newer is required.
* Compatibility depends on the FM hardware, vendor drivers, and firmware included by the device manufacturer or custom ROM. A Qualcomm Snapdragon processor alone does not guarantee support.
* The app supports Qualcomm devices exposing either the legacy V4L2 radio interface or the newer FM HAL.
* Wired headphones not required as an FM antenna.

**Features:**

* Live FM playback without an Internet connection.
* Manual tuning, hardware seek, and full-band station scanning.
* Real-time signal strength and stereo/mono indicators.
* RDS and RBDS metadata, including PS, RT, PTY, PI, country, and alternative frequencies when provided by the station and supported by the driver.
* Europe, United States, Japan Standard, and Japan Wide tuner regions.
* Favorites with custom station names, multiple lists, drag-and-drop reordering, and ZIP import/export.
* Recording to 16-bit PCM WAV or MP3 at 128/192 kbit/s.
* Optional five-second recording pre-roll.
